# Proprio

> Proprio is a Seattle-based surgical technology company modernizing how surgery is predicted, performed, and perfected. Its FDA-cleared Paradigm platform fuses proprietary light-field imaging, an advanced sensor array (Prism), and artificial intelligence to build a continuous, real-time 3D model of anatomy during spine and orthopedic procedures — delivering radiation-free intraoperative measurement, per-vertebra registration, and structured surgical data capture. Founded in 2016 and backed by DCVC. Proprio is a hardware- and software-based medical device company and does not currently publish a public developer API, SDK, or developer portal.

## Products
- Paradigm: FDA-cleared AI-driven surgical navigation and visualization platform for spine and orthopedic surgery.
- Paradigm OS: Surgeon-led, AI-enabled intelligence system for surgical performance.
- Prism: Light-field-based multi-modal vision sensor array (optical, depth, and infrared tracking).

## Developer Surface
- No public developer API, SDK, CLI, or developer portal is currently published. Data integrations (e.g. surgical registry integration) are handled through private partnerships.
